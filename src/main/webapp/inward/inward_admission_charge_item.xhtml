• Each row here is a routine charge that is billed automatically to every matching admission, on top of the room and service charges - these charges are additive, never a replacement for anything else.
  • Resolution for one item happens in two steps: first by Admission Type, then by Payment Method. A blank Admission Type means "applies to every admission type"; a blank Payment Method means "applies to both Cash and Credit".
  • The trap: as soon as you add one Admission-Type-specific row for an item, the blank ("Any Admission Type") rows stop applying to that admission type entirely. If you configure a charge for, say, Day Case admissions, you must configure it for both Cash and Credit under Day Case, or one of them will silently get no charge at all for this item.
  • An item used here must already have a Department, an Institution, an Inward Charge Type and a live Fee configured under Item administration - the Save button checks all four before accepting a row.
