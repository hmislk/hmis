Billed income for the period, by bill date, net of cancellations and refunds. Services are OPD bills grouped by the department that performs each service (Laboratory, ECG, X-Ray, Dressing, ...). Channelling shows the doctor fee and hospital fee; only the hospital fee counts toward the grand total. Wards is taken from the final bills of patients discharged in the period, by admitting department. Collecting Centres shows the bill value per centre. Institution, Site and Department filter the billing department (for Wards, the admitting department).
