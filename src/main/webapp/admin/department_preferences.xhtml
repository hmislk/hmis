About Department Preferences

Department preferences let each department independently enable or disable specific behaviors, instead of relying on a single system-wide setting. Changes made here only affect the #{departmentPreferencesController.loggedUserDepartmentName} department and take effect immediately after saving.

Allow Disposal Issue to Same Department: When enabled, this department is allowed to create a pharmacy disposal issue where the issuing and receiving department are the same. When disabled (the system default), such issues are blocked.
