# Client Portal Registration: Business Rules

Applies to patient registration through the online portal, the mobile app and the kiosk. Online payment, receipts and authorized-person registration are separate features.

## Rules
1. A patient starts by entering a phone number. An OTP sent to that number must be confirmed before anything else is shown.
2. After the OTP, the patients already saved under that phone number are listed. The patient picks one and creates a user account to manage it.
3. If no patient exists under that phone number, the patient enters their details and a new patient is created.
4. Every new patient gets a PHN at once, also while the registration awaits approval.
5. A PHN is generated by the system. Nobody types or edits it during registration.
6. One user account manages one patient. A person cannot hold two accounts.
7. Each hospital sets whether new registrations need staff approval, through an Application Option. It is off by default.
8. When approval is required, the patient sees only the approval status until staff approve. Booking and records stay hidden.
9. When approval is not required, the records linked to the patient under that phone number become available soon after registration.
10. Only staff with the approval privilege can approve a registration. Each approval records who approved and when.
11. A lost phone is handled by the hospital as an emergency theft outside this feature. The portal has no self-service recovery for it.

## Configuration and privileges
- Application Option (Administration → Manage Institutions → Preferences → Application Options): require staff approval of new registrations. The key name says what true does; existing Client Portal keys keep their meaning.
- Privilege: approve portal registrations (name set following the security-privileges conventions).
